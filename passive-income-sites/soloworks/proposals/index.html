FAQ

Proposal questions freelancers actually ask.

What should a freelance proposal include?

Six things: the client's goal in their own words, concrete deliverables, a timeline, a fixed price with deposit terms, explicit exclusions, and a clear next step. That's exactly the structure this generator builds. Long context-setting sections and boilerplate "about me" pages mostly go unread.

How long should it be?

For most projects under ~$10K, one page. It reads as confidence and gets a same-day decision more often than a deck. Save multi-page proposals for bigger engagements with committees — our Proposal Pack includes both lengths.

Should I show one price or options?

Three options usually outperforms one: most clients pick the middle, so make the middle the project you want. This generator produces the single-price version; the three-option pricing table (with the anchoring math explained) is in the Proposal Pack.

Is my proposal data uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your sender name is stored in your own browser's localStorage so you don't retype it; nothing is ever sent to a server.
