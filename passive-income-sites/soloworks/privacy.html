Privacy Policy & Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: 4 July 2026

The short version

SoloWorks is a static website. We don't run user accounts, we don't set tracking cookies of our own, and our free tools process everything on your device. We make money from affiliate links and product sales, both disclosed below.

What our tools do with your data

The rate calculator and invoice generator run entirely in your browser. The numbers, names and invoice details you enter are never transmitted to us or anyone else. To save you re-typing, the invoice generator stores your own sender details in your browser's local storage, on your device, where only you can access it. Clearing your browser data removes it completely.

What we collect

  • Server logs. Our hosting provider (Netlify) records standard technical logs (IP address, pages requested, browser type) to serve and secure the site. See Netlify's own privacy policy for details.
  • Nothing else. No analytics profiles, no advertising cookies, no email lists — unless we add a newsletter later, in which case subscribing will be explicit and opt-in, and this policy will be updated.

Affiliate disclosure

Some outbound links on this site — particularly in the tool directory and on the calculator page — are affiliate links. If you click one and later sign up or purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

Our promise: affiliate status never determines what we list or what we say about it. Tools are included because they're genuinely useful; several tools we list have no affiliate program at all. Sponsored placements (the "Featured" cards in the directory) are always labeled as such.

Purchases

Template purchases are processed by our payment partner (e.g. Gumroad), which handles your payment details under its own privacy policy — we never see your card information.

Third-party links

Links to other websites are provided in good faith, but we're not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact option in the site footer or the sponsor-inquiry channel on the tools page.
