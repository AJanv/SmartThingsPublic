FAQ

What format are the templates in?

Every pack includes Google Docs links (File → Make a copy), a Notion duplicate link, and print-ready PDFs. Nothing requires special software.

Can I use them for client work?

Yes — that's the point. Unlimited personal and commercial use for your own freelance business. The only thing you can't do is resell or redistribute the templates themselves.

Are the contract materials legal advice?

No. The Contract Companion is an educational checklist with example wording to bring to a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction — it is not a substitute for legal advice.
