FAQ

Straight answers to the questions freelancers actually ask.

How do I calculate my freelance hourly rate?

Add your target annual income and your yearly business expenses, gross it up for tax, then divide by your true billable hours: working weeks × hours per week × the percentage of time you can actually bill (for most freelancers that's 50–70%, the rest goes to admin, marketing and proposals). Finally add a 10–20% buffer for slow months. That's exactly what the calculator above does.

Why should my rate be higher than an employee salary?

Because you're paying for everything an employer normally covers: taxes, insurance, retirement, equipment, software, holidays and sick days — plus the unbillable hours you spend finding and managing clients. A common rule of thumb is 1.5×–2× the equivalent employee hourly wage.

Is my invoice data uploaded anywhere?

No. Both tools run entirely in your browser. Your "from" details are saved to your own device (localStorage) so you don't retype them, and nothing is ever sent to a server.

How do I save the invoice as a PDF?

Click Print / Save as PDF and choose "Save as PDF" as the destination in your browser's print dialog. The page is styled so only the invoice prints.
